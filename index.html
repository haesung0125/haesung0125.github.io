Hae Seong Lee, Ph.D.

Postdoctoral researcher · SPEED Lab, Department of Physics, Inha University · haesung0125@gmail.com

I am a postdoctoral researcher studying how collective behavior emerges from simple interactions in complex systems. My research spans synchronization of coupled oscillators, dynamics on complex networks, and ecological communities, using tools from statistical physics and large-scale simulation.
